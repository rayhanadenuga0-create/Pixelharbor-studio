## Discovery questions
1. What is the main goal: more enquiries, bookings, sales or a more professional presence?
2. Who is the target customer?
3. What pages, content or features are needed?
4. Who supplies text, photos and branding?
5. What is the deadline?
6. What is the budget range?
7. Who owns the domain, hosting and accounts?

## Before starting paid work
Send a written scope covering:
- Deliverables and exclusions
- Price and currency
- Deposit/milestones and final payment
- Timeline and what the client must provide
- Number of revision rounds
- Hosting, domain, plugins and recurring costs
- Handover, ownership and support period

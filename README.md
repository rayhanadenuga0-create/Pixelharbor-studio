## Free-first launch checklist
1. Preview `index.html` locally.
2. Publish the static page using GitHub Pages or another free static host. A custom domain is optional and usually costs money.
3. Test the enquiry link on both desktop and mobile.
4. Create 3 clearly labelled concept demos (for example: cafe landing page, barber booking page, local service business homepage). Never present concept work as paid client work.
5. Contact a small, relevant batch of businesses with a personalised observation and a specific offer.
6. Only begin client work after agreeing scope, price, deadline, revision limit and payment milestones.

## Customer acquisition
- Start with one niche at a time: cafes, barbers, tutors, tradespeople or independent shops.
- Find businesses with outdated sites, broken mobile layouts, unclear contact details or inconsistent social graphics.
- Send 10 personalised messages per day, track replies and follow up once after 4–5 days.
- Offer a small, useful sample (such as a homepage redesign concept), not a full unpaid website.
- Ask permission before using a business logo, photos or name in a public demo.
- Do not spam, scrape private contact details or make fake claims about results.
